Sources and methods
Live vehicles
BKK FUTÁR API, vehicles within 20 km of the centre, read every 10 seconds through a small proxy (the API key is not in this page). Markers glide between readings. Direction from the feed; where missing, from the last two positions. Metro is not in the live feed. Delay is not in this feed.
Colours
One colour per mode for vehicles and timetable lines. Metro lines keep their official M1–M4 colours. The line's own colour appears on the badge in the popup.
Timetable lines and stops
BKK GTFS 2768.20260926, weekday, one shape per line; stops sized by weekday departures.
Base map
© OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL): districts (land only), Danube, main roads, railway lines and stations. City boundary derived from districts + Danube.
Library
Built on Budapest Map Library v2.15 (27 Sep 2026), read only.

Live and timetable data: Data source: BKK Zrt., CC BY 4.0. Independent analysis; not produced, reviewed or endorsed by BKK.
